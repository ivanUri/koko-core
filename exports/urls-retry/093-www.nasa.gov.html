Roscosmos Soyuz MS-28

NASA astronaut Chris Williams and Roscosmos cosmonauts Sergey Kud‑Sverchkov and Sergei Mikaev are concluding their 241‑day mission aboard the International Space Station. The crew will undock from the station on Sunday, July 26, heading for a parachute-assisted landing on the steppe of Kazakhstan, southeast of Dzhezkazgan. NASA’s live coverage continues at 2:30 a.m. EDT.
